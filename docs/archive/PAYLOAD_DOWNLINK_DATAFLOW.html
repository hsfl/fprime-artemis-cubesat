Per 1 Hz tick: retries first (RETRY_PACKETS_PER_RUN=1, preempts fresh data), then fresh (PACKETS_PER_RUN=1). Events: Started · Progress (every 10%) · RetryRequested · Complete (summary ×3) · Failed. Telemetry every 5 ticks while active, every 30 idle.
